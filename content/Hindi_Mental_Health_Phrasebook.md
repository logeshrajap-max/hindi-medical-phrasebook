# 2. DEPRESSION SCREENING (अवसाद)

### PHQ-2 Equivalent Questions

| English | Hindi (Devanagari) | Romanization |
|---------|-------------------|--------------|
| Over the last 2 weeks, how often have you been bothered by little interest or pleasure in doing things? | पिछले 2 हफ्तों में, आपको काम में मन कम लगता है? ![[Hindi_Audio/audio_0864.mp3]] | Pichle do hafton mein, aapko kaam mein mann kam lagta hai? |
| Over the last 2 weeks, how often have you been bothered by feeling down, depressed, or hopeless? | पिछले 2 हफ्तों में, आप उदास, निराश या बेबस महसूस करते हैं? ![[Hindi_Audio/audio_0865.mp3]] | Pichle do hafton mein, aap udaas, niraash ya bebass mehsoos karte hain? |

| Have you been having thoughts of hurting yourself? | क्या आप खुद को नुकसान पहुँचाने के बारे में सोचते हैं? ![[Hindi_Audio/audio_0867.mp3]] | Kya aap khud ko nukesaan pahunchaane ke baare mein sochte hain? |
