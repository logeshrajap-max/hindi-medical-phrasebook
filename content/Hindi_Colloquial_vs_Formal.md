# 1. THE THREE "YOU" FORMS IN HINDI (आप / तुम / तू)

| Form | Hindi | When to use | Example |
|------|-------|-------------|---------|
| **Aap (आप)** — Formal & Respectful | आप ![[Hindi_Audio/audio_1619.mp3]] | Elders, patients, strangers, professional settings. ALWAYS safe with patients. | आप कैसे हैं? (Aap kaise hain?) ![[Hindi_Audio/audio_1620.mp3]] |
| **Tum (तुम)** — Familiar | तुम ![[Hindi_Audio/audio_1621.mp3]] | Peers, younger people, acquaintances, children. Sometimes used with patients but less respectful. | तुम कैसे हो? (Tum kaise ho?) ![[Hindi_Audio/audio_1622.mp3]] |
| **Tu (तू)** — Intimate/Casual | तू ![[Hindi_Audio/audio_1623.mp3]] | Close friends, children, sometimes family. Avoid with patients (rude). | तू कैसा है? (Tu kaisa hai?) ![[Hindi_Audio/audio_1624.mp3]] |

> **💡 Important:** For ALL patient communication, use **आप (aap)**. It conveys respect. Avoid **तुम (tum)** and **तू (tu)** with adult patients — many find it disrespectful.

# 7. EASIEST WAY TO COMMUNICATE (सबसे आसान तरीका)

### Recommended Approach for Medical Settings

1. **Use simple everyday Hindi** — not heavy Sanskrit-based words.
2. **Mix in English medical terms** — Indian patients commonly understand "fever", "BP", "sugar", "operation", "injection", "medicine".
3. **Use the "aap" form** always — stays respectful.
4. **Use polite commands** ("baithiye", "kholiye", "lijiye").
5. **Confirm understanding** — ask patient to repeat back.

### Example: A natural, easy sentence
- **English:** "Sit down, I will check your blood pressure."
- **Hindi:** "बैठिए, मैं आपका ब्लड प्रेशर जाँचूंगा।"
- **Romanization:** "Baithiye, main aapka blood pressure jaanchunga."
