A free, complete Hindi medical phrasebook for **Tamil/English-speaking doctors**. Every entry has **English → Hindi (Devanagari) → Romanization**, plus a **playable audio pronunciation** for each Hindi phrase.

Built by a medical student to help doctors talk with Hindi-speaking patients.

## 🔊 About the Audio

- **1704 audio clips** covering every Hindi phrase in the set.
- Natural **Hindi female neural voice**.
- Click the ▶ player next to any Hindi phrase to hear it.
- Great for practising pronunciation before a consultation or checking a phrase on the spot.

## 🎯 How to Use

1. Open the **Main Phrasebook** for general history-taking & examination.
2. Open the **specialty file** matching your patient.
3. Tap the **audio player** to hear correct pronunciation.
4. Always use the respectful **"आप" (aap)** form with patients (see Colloquial vs Formal + Cultural Notes).
